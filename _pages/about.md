I am a PhD student at NC State University studying machine learning. Driven by the conviction that mathematics is simultaneously useful and beautiful, I enjoy doing research that is both practically relevant and theoretically rigorous. My research focus is in formal privacy definitions, such as differential privacy and its variants, and how these definitions can be used in a machine learning setting. Practical concerns drive what kind of privacy we are interested in and feasible implementation constraints. Theoretical rigor allows formal privacy guarantees and provides methods to improve privacy-utility tradeoffs. I have also done work in the interaction between pretraining and fine-tuning for large language models.
